# Stage 18, Lesson 2 — Approval-review training prototype

These local static pages are the exact synthetic teaching prototype used in the repaired lesson captures. They are not a deployed HarborDesk product or a record of a real approval or send.

Open each file locally in a desktop browser, in order:

1. `approval-summary.html`: the inadequate “Routine follow-up for HD-204” summary, with Approve disabled. The original authorization and complete approved body remain visible.
2. `approval-collapsed.html`: the proposed `send_message` record with recipient and body values initially collapsed. The browser's native disclosure controls only show or hide local text.
3. `approval-consequence.html`: the full pending arguments and the statement of what approval would permit.

The lesson captures use actual Firefox 128.0 ESR on Ubuntu 24.04, English, light theme. The supplied authorized note says “Ticket HD-204 is pending review. No further action is requested.” The pending recipient and body are deliberately different. The approved body is taken from the complete saved lesson narration, not the earlier faulty visual placeholders.

Every address is reserved example data. The files contain no JavaScript, form submission, external resource or send capability; their content-security policy disables external loading. Opening a page or a disclosure does not approve or execute an action. No review decision is prefilled. This is an inspection exercise, not production authorization, authentication or a message-delivery implementation.

Compare the recipient, full body and possible consequence against the exact user authorization. Retain the separation between a proposal, approval, execution and a lasting effect in your own worksheet.
